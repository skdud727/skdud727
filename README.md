## About Me

I am an M.S. student in Computer Science at **KAIST**, advised by **Prof. Jun Han** in the CyPhy Lab. My research focuses on **preserving people's unique styles through computational technologies** and **expanding accessibility to art**.

I am particularly interested in building **AI systems that protect people while supporting their creativity and productivity** on digital platforms. Before KAIST, I received my B.S. in Computer Engineering from DGIST (Cum Laude) and worked as a research intern at the University of Tokyo (Igarashi Lab), KAIST (KIXLAB), and DGIST.
